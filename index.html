JukeBox for Root – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Summary: JukeBox keeps only what it needs to run a shared music queue. That is the YouTube links people add and which Root member added them. It keeps this in memory only. It does not sell or share data, run analytics or show ads of its own.

1. Who we are

JukeBox is an independent App for the Root community platform, operated by Daniel Atanasov. JukeBox is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube, Google or Root Communications, Inc.

2. Data JukeBox processes

  • Queue content: the YouTube video IDs and titles that members add to the queue.
  • Root member ID: the Root user ID of the member who added a track or used a control (play, pause, skip). JukeBox uses it to show activity in the channel, such as "user …1234 added a track."
  • Playback state: the current track, play/pause state and position, so all members stay in sync.

JukeBox does not collect names, email addresses, messages, voice or video, location or payment information.

3. How long data is kept

Queue and playback data live in the App server's memory for the channel where JukeBox is installed. They are deleted when the queue is cleared, when the App restarts, or when the App is removed from the community. JukeBox keeps no long-term copy.

4. Third-party services

  • YouTube: videos play through the official YouTube embedded player (IFrame Player API). When it loads, YouTube may collect data and set cookies under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • YouTube oEmbed: the App server requests public video titles from YouTube's oEmbed endpoint. These requests contain only the video ID.
  • Root: JukeBox runs on Root's infrastructure. Root's own privacy policy covers your Root account.

5. Sharing

JukeBox shows the queue and activity only to members of the channel where it is installed. Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

6. Children

JukeBox is not directed at children under the minimum age Root requires for its service.

7. Your choices

Community administrators can clear the queue at any time or remove JukeBox from the community, which deletes its data. For questions or deletion requests, contact us below.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
